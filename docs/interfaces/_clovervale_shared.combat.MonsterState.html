    Readonlyaction

    action: "Idle" | "Walk" | "Die" | "Attack" | "Sit"

    What the monster is doing.

    ReadonlyactionCount

    actionCount: number

    The number of actions the monster has started so far. It changes whenever the monster starts an action, even the same one again.

    Readonlyhealth

    health: number

    The health of the monster, from 0 to its full health.

    Readonlyid

    id: number

    The identifier of the monster, unique among the monsters of its world.

    Optional ReadonlyisArmed

    isArmed?: boolean

    Whether the monster holds its weapon now, for a kind that holds one now and then, as MonsterKind.hasWeapon tells, which is left out for any other kind.

    Readonlykind

    kind: "village-dog" | "cave-dog" | "martha"

    The kind of the monster, which tells its full health.

    Readonlymode

    mode: MonsterMode

    What the monster is up to, among the modes of its kind.

    Readonlyx

    x: number

    The position along the X axis, in meters.

    Readonlyyaw

    yaw: number

    The turn around the Y axis from facing +Z toward +X, in degrees.

    Readonlyz

    z: number

    The position along the Z axis, in meters.
