    Interface TrailingCameraSettings

    How the owner has the camera turn after the player it follows.

    interface TrailingCameraSettings {
        angleLimit: number;
        deadZone: number;
        delay: number;
        easeTime: number;
        isEnabled: boolean;
        turnRate: number;
    }

    Properties

    angleLimit

    angleLimit: number

    How far the way the player goes may be from the way the camera looks for the camera to turn after it at all, in degrees. The camera turns ever more weakly as the way the player goes nears this angle.

    deadZone

    deadZone: number

    How far the way the player goes may be from the way the camera looks before the camera starts to turn, in degrees.

    delay

    delay: number

    How long the player has to go a way the camera may turn after, with the camera left alone, before the camera starts to turn, in seconds.

    easeTime

    easeTime: number

    How long the camera takes to get from standing to its fastest turn, and back, in seconds.

    isEnabled

    isEnabled: boolean

    Whether the camera comes around behind the way the player goes, rather than keeping its heading.

    turnRate

    turnRate: number

    The fastest the camera turns, in degrees per second.
