    populate: (models: readonly LoadedModel[]) => void

    Stands the models that have a place in the village there, as far as they are among the models, each idling if it is animated, scatters the clover cover over the open grass around the village if the patch of clover is among them, and starts the light in the windows of the houses shown and of the fortress far off, and the glows of the fountain and the forge, as far as their models are among them.
