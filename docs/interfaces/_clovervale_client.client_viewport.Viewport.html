    ReadonlyswitchScene

    switchScene: (
        scene: Scene,
        focus: GroundVector,
        heading: number,
        leastElevation: number,
    ) => void

    Shows another scene from now on, with the camera at once on what it looks at there, looking along a heading, at the distance it had, and looking down on it at SWITCH_ELEVATION at least, so that what stands around it hides it seldom. The trailing camera starts afresh, so that nothing it did in the scene before carries over.
