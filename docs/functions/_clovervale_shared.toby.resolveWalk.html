    Function resolveWalk

    • resolveWalk(
          from: GroundVector,
          to: GroundVector,
          toby: GroundVector,
          terrain: Terrain,
      ): GroundVector

      Finds where a player that walks from one point toward another ends up, with the obstacles and Toby in its way.

      Toby stops the player as the obstacles do: a player that walks into him at an angle slides around him, and one that walks straight into him stops. A player that overlaps him already, having had no room to be pushed out of his way, may walk anywhere that does not take it farther into him.

      Parameters

      • from: GroundVector

        Where the player stands, clear of the obstacles.

      • to: GroundVector

        Where the player would be with nothing in its way.

      • toby: GroundVector

        Where Toby is.

      • terrain: Terrain

        The ground the player walks on.

      Returns GroundVector

      Where the player ends up, clear of the obstacles and within the boundary.
