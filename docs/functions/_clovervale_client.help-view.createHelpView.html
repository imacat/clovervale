    Function createHelpView

    • createHelpView(
          parent: HTMLElement,
          translator: Translator,
          sheet: HelpSheet,
          clickButton: () => void,
          close: () => void,
          moveOn: () => void,
          isTouch: () => boolean,
      ): HelpView

      Creates the help sheet of the page: its button right of the icon of the bag, a piece of parchment with a question mark, with the key that opens it, H, on it, and, while the sheet is open, the sheet itself in the middle of the page, with its title, under it the name of its page and a dot for each page, the dot of the page shown marked, between arrows that turn to the page before and after, the controls of the page, each with its keys and what it does, a cross that closes it at its top right, a button that closes it at its foot, and under that, in smaller words, a line with links to the source code of the game and to its documentation for developers, which open in a new tab and leave the sheet as it is, as clicked or tapped, or with Enter while they have the focus. The first page tells the keyboard and the mouse, the second the gamepad, its buttons drawn as on a Logitech F310, and the third the touch controls, drawn as small pictures; while the sheet offers the credits of the game, a fourth page tells them, and before that there is no trace of it. While the sheet shows the welcome to the game, a smaller sheet of the same parchment stands in the middle of the page in its place, with the welcome as its title, a few lines under it that tell the world the player has entered, and at its foot, in smaller words, a line that tells to press any key or, on a touch device, to tap to go on, and a click or a tap anywhere on the page outside the panel moves on to the sheet. While the sheet is open, no click on the page outside the panel reaches the game underneath. What the sheet shows is worded again in each language the page comes to speak.

      Parameters

      • parent: HTMLElement

        What the sheet is added to.

      • translator: Translator

        The translator of the text of the page.

      • sheet: HelpSheet

        The help sheet.

      • clickButton: () => void

        Called when the button is clicked.

      • close: () => void

        Called when the cross or the button at the foot of the sheet is clicked.

      • moveOn: () => void

        Called when the page is clicked while the sheet shows the welcome.

      • isTouch: () => boolean

        Tells whether the page is used by touch, so that the line at the foot of the welcome tells to tap rather than to press a key.

      Returns HelpView

      The sheet as shown.
