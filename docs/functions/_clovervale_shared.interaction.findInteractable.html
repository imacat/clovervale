    Function findInteractable

    • findInteractable(
          pose: Pose,
          step: number,
          surroundings: Surroundings,
          input?: InteractionInput,
      ): Interactable | null

      Finds what a player uses when it interacts with the world at a step of the simulation, standing as it does in a map, with the monsters and what there is to pick there as they are.

      Something is within reach of the player if the player stands within its near reach of its center, whichever way the player faces, or within its reach of its center, with the center no more than INTERACTION_HALF_ANGLE to either side of the way the player faces: a fountain and a way into another map within their reaches, as WorldMap.fountains and WorldMap.passages tell; a villager within TALK_REACH of where the villager is at the step; a four-leaf clover in a patch, or an item on the ground, within PICK_REACH, or within FEET_REACH whichever way the player faces; and a chest or a barrel in the cave within LOOT_REACH.

      The fountains and the ways of each map are its own, as WorldMap tells, and each villager is in its own map: the prisoner, the only one in the cave, is talked to through the bars of his cell. Martha, who fights as a monster, stands where she is among the monsters, if she is among them, and cannot be talked to while she is not free, as isVillagerFree tells. The patches of clover are in the village alone, and the chests and the barrels in the cave alone.

      Of what is within reach, the fountain comes first, then the villagers, in the order VILLAGERS tells them, then the ways into the other maps, and last what there is to pick: the nearest of the clovers, the items and the containers that hold something, an item or silver coins, a clover before an item as near and an item before a container, or else the nearest of the containers that hold nothing or have been looted. A villager and a way give way to a slash at a monster in a fight, as isFighting tells, that the slash would hit, as isWithinStrike tells with SLASH_REACH. Whether the player has room for what it picks is not for this to tell.

      Parameters

      • pose: Pose

        Where the player stands, and which way it faces.

      • step: number

        The number of the step.

      • surroundings: Surroundings

        The map the player is in, the monsters there, and what there is to pick there.

      • Optionalinput: InteractionInput

        The input the player uses it with, if only what that input uses is to be found, which an interaction still comes before; any input by default, as the space bar uses it.

      Returns Interactable | null

      The first of what is within reach, or null if there is none, or if it is used with another input than the one asked for.
