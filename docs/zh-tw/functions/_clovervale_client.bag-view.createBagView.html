    Function createBagView

    • createBagView(
          parent: HTMLElement,
          translator: Translator,

      Creates the bag of the player of the page: its icon in the bottom left corner, or the top left on a touch device, with the key that opens it, I, on it, and next to it, while the bag is open, its title, with the silver coins of the player in the corner beside it, drawn as a small silver coin followed by their number, none as well, its slots in a square grid, each item drawn small as what it is, with its cursor, the menu of the item the cursor is on while it is open, with the actions that cannot be chosen now grayed out, a line under the slots that names the item the cursor is on, blank for an empty slot, and a line that tells the keys, or what to tap on a touch device. A click on a slot or an action of the menu does to the bag what InventoryBag tells, and a click on the icon is left to the caller; no click on any part of the bag reaches the game underneath. What the bag shows is worded again in each language the page comes to speak.

      Parameters

      • parent: HTMLElement

        What the bag is added to.

      • translator: Translator

        The translator of the text of the page.

      • bag: InventoryBag

        The bag.

      • carryOut: (request: ItemRequest) => void

        Carries out an action chosen in the menu of an item.

      • clickIcon: () => void

        Called when the icon is clicked.

      • isTouch: () => boolean

        Tells whether the page is used by touch, so that the line under the slots tells what to tap rather than the keys.

      Returns BagView

      The bag as shown.
