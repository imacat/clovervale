    Class HelpSheet

    The help sheet: whether it is open, the page it is open at, and whether it shows the welcome to the game before its pages.

    The H key opens the sheet, at the page of the keyboard. While it is open, Esc, Enter and the H key close it, the left and right arrows turn to the page before and after, stopping at the first and the last, and it takes every other key of the game too, so that nothing else on the page does anything with it, and leaves every other key to the browser. While it shows the welcome, any key pressed, but for one that repeats, moves on to its pages instead, the sheet staying open.
