    Class PushForecast

    Draws the player of the page pushed out of the way of the pusher of its map, the villager there who pushes the players aside, as findPusher tells, as the pusher is drawn, while the player rests, before the server tells of the push.

    The page leaves it to the server to push a player that rests, and learns of each push only from a snapshot, while the pusher runs on as it is drawn. The forecast draws a player only while it rests, and only once a snapshot has told the page so: from the place and the step the snapshot tells, until the page moves the player on by a step of its own, the server pushes it by the rule the page knows, with the pusher where its course has it at each of its steps. The forecast draws the player at the places the server pushes it to as the pusher is drawn: between the place after the step the pusher is drawn at and the place after the next, as far toward the latter as it takes to keep clear of the pusher, but never back along those places, and no faster than PUSH_SPEED_RATIO times as fast as the pusher runs, setting out from where the snapshot has it. A snapshot that tells of a push the forecast foresaw leaves the player drawn where it was. A resting player the pusher does not push is drawn just as without the forecast.

    So, while the player rests, it is drawn only at places the server has it at, or between two it has it at one step after the other, at a step no later than the one after the step the pusher is drawn at, and it never moves back toward the run of the pusher but as the server moves it. It reaches into the pusher only for the first few frames the pusher touches it, as the pusher pushes it faster than the forecast draws it, and where the server itself leaves it reaching into the pusher, by a wall or the fence.

    As the player sets off, starts to interact or dies, or as the owner switches the forecast or the smoothing of the corrections off, the forecast hands over: the player glides once from where it was drawn to where it is drawn without the forecast, which it reaches SMOOTHING_DURATION later, never going beyond both, and it is drawn just as without the forecast from then on, until it rests again and a snapshot tells so. A snapshot that tells of the player resting elsewhere than the forecast foresaw hands over likewise, from where it was drawn to where the snapshot has it. A handover is drawn as a jump when it moves the player SMOOTHING_LIMIT or more, when the frame it comes at is drawn more than a few frames' time after the one before, and when the snapshot that tells of the player resting elsewhere shows it has done something else since the frame before, such as dying and coming back to life. None is drawn while the owner has the player shown without prediction, when it is drawn without the forecast.

    While the server stays silent, the forecast foresees the pushes for FORECAST_HORIZON after the step it last told of, and holds the player where it has it then.

    What the forecast gives up: the prediction knows nothing of the pushes the forecast has drawn until a snapshot tells of them, so a player that sets off drawn pushed glides back toward where the prediction has it, and the push of the server then shows as a correction, as without the forecast. A player that has just stopped is drawn as without the forecast, reaching into the pusher if it runs into the player, until a snapshot tells the page that it rests. The forecast cannot foresee what only the server does: a player killed as it is pushed may have been drawn pushed farther than the server pushed it, and glides back to where it lies. A player the owner switches the forecast off and on again for as the pusher pushes it glides back to where it is drawn without the forecast, and out again. While the step the pusher is drawn at lags behind that of the server, as its drawn clock catches up at a tenth of its pace after the latency falls sharply, a player the snapshots already tell pushed is still drawn where it was until the drawn pusher reaches it: with Toby, up to about a second after a fall from 1000 ms to none, 0.3 s after one from 300 ms to 20 ms, and up to 0.39 m away. And as nothing is judged from where the player is drawn, the prompts for what it can interact with go by where it is not yet pushed for longer than without the forecast.

    Only where the player is drawn changes: it faces the way it truly faces, doing what it truly does, and nothing it does is judged from where it is drawn.

          smoothing: CorrectionSmoothing,
          time: number,
          map: "village" | "cave",
          shownStep: number | null,
      ): PlayerMotion

      Moves the forecast on to a frame, and tells the player as it is to be drawn in it. It is to be told of every frame, and only once.

      Parameters

      • prediction: PlayerPrediction

        The prediction of the player, brought up to the time of the frame.

      • smoothing: CorrectionSmoothing

        The smoothing of its corrections, which this tells of the frame as well.

      • time: number

        The time of the frame on the clock of the page, in milliseconds, which is no earlier than any time told before.

      • map: "village" | "cave"

        The map the player is drawn in, which tells the villager who pushes the players aside there, if any, as findPusher tells.

      • shownStep: number | null

        The step of the server the villagers are drawn at in the frame, which may fall between two, or null if they are not drawn.

      Returns PlayerMotion

      The player as it is to be drawn: as the smoothing draws it, unless the forecast draws it elsewhere.
