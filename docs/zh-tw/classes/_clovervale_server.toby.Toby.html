    Class Toby

    Toby, the child who hunts for four-leaf clover, who runs his course as the number of the step tells. At a step at which his hand grabs at a patch of clover, he takes the four-leaf clover of the patch if it has its clover, as what there is to pick in the world tells, and exclaims TOBY_FOUND_LINE in passing as he does, which every player of the world within hearing of him then hears, and nobody else.
