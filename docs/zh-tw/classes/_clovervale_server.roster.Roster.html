    create

    • create(elsewhere: readonly number[]): Player

      Lets a new player in.

      The player gets an identifier no player of the world has had before, the appearance the fewest players have, here and elsewhere (the lowest-numbered one if several tie), and a place at a spawn point, as Roster.chooseSpawnPoint tells, where it idles facing the fountain, at full health, carrying nothing and with no silver coins, in no dialogue, told by no villager who it is, on no quest, and not hunted by Martha.

      Parameters

      • elsewhere: readonly number[]

        The appearances of the players in other worlds.
