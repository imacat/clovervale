    Class SpeechBubble

    The bubble that shows what the villager last talked to says, and a bubble for each player that shows what it last said.

    • fitView(width: number, height: number, fieldOfView: number): void

      Takes in the view the bubbles are seen in, and draws the bubbles shown, if any, and those to come, at the size for it: as painted in a view at least 600 pixels tall, and as large as in a view that tall in a shorter one, as on a phone held on its side. In a narrow view, as on a phone held upright, a bubble is no wider than 85% of the view, unless a single word is, its words wrapped onto more lines instead.

    say

    • say(villager: Villager, head: Vector3, lineId?: PlainMessageId | null): void

      Shows what a villager says, in the language of the page, in a bubble just above its head, for as long as measureSpeechDuration tells, in place of any bubble of a villager shown before. The bubbles of the players stay as they are.

      A villager says the line it is given, or else, with several lines, one of them, picked at random, but never the line it said the time before, save that one who greets first says its first line the first time. It is called by the name it goes by once it has told the player of the page who it is, if it has, as noticeAcquaintances was last told.

      Parameters

      • villager: Villager

        The villager.

      • head: Vector3

        The top of the head of the villager.

      • OptionallineId: PlainMessageId | null = null

        The message of the line it says, or null for one of its own lines, which it is by default.

      Returns void
