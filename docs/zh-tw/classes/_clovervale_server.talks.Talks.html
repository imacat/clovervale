    Class Talks

    The talks of the players with the villagers of a world: the lines the villagers say, the replies of the players, and what the lines bring about.

    Whenever a line is said to a player, what the line brings about comes about at once: the villager may tell the player who it is; may take an item of the kind it names out of the inventory of the player, the items after it closing up; may take as many of its silver coins as it names, or all it has if it has fewer; may then give it an item, which is laid at its feet as if put down if its inventory has no room for it; and may then tell its listeners of an event of the story of the player, as StoryTeller.tell tells.

    start

    • start(player: Player, villager: VillagerId): void

      Starts a talk of a player to a villager.

      A talk to a villager whose words the server chooses for the player, as hasServerWords tells, opens with the first of the openings of its script whose condition holds of the player, as findOpening finds, or else with a line of its chatter, as chooseChatter chooses, if any: a line said in passing is kept for the player to be told once, and any other line starts a dialogue. Any other talk changes nothing.

      Parameters

      • player: Player

        The player.

      • villager: VillagerId

        The villager.

      Returns void
