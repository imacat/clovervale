    Class ClovervaleQuest

    The main plot, which a player opens the first time it goes down into the cave: it has two goals, which the player may reach in either order, the message of Edwin, as he gives it, and the token of Martha, as the player beats her, and is done once it has reached both. Until the player has beaten her, it asks Martha for the player whenever it climbs out of the well: as having found the prisoner, or, once it has the message, as holding it.
