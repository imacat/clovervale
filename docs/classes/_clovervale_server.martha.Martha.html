    Class Martha

    Martha, the village chief, who is the enemy spy, as the server moves her among the players of her map, with the numbers MONSTER_KINDS tells of her.

    She deals with a player she is told needs her, as Martha.request tells, and with nobody else but those she hunts, as she sees fit for what it needs of her; she hunts a player she loses in a fight, or that leaves her map while she fights it, until it beats her; and a player she comes up to is in a dialogue with her from then on, as the talks of her map tell. A player in any other dialogue with her leaves it as soon as she is no longer free to be talked to, as isVillagerFree tells. She tells her listeners of the player that beats her, as BaseResident.tell tells, as an event of type martha-beaten.

    She deals with one player at a time, and the others she is to deal with wait in the order they came. A player she is told needs her, and that she does not hunt, waits as long as it stays on her map; any other waits as long as it stays in her area, no farther from her home than her leash. She passes over a waiting player while it is dead or out of her area, and turns to it once it is alive and back, if she is free. To a player that has found the prisoner in her cave, or that holds his message, and that she does not hunt, she walks up, around the obstacles, and once near enough opens a dialogue with it; once the dialogue is over, she fights it. A player she hunts she fights at once, without a word, and she hunts a player she notices, within her notice, in her area. Whenever she sets out to a player without her pitchfork, she first takes it up from the wall of her house, at PITCHFORK_STAND, and she comes up to the player, and fights it, only with it in her hands; and whenever she walks back home with it, she first leans it against the wall again. In a fight, she chases the player and thrusts her pitchfork at it whenever near enough, until it gets out of her area or dies, when she hunts it from then on, or until it beats her, when she drops her pitchfork beside her and sits on the ground for a while, then gets up at full health and takes it up again. Whenever a fight ends with her not beaten, she is at full health again at once. A player who dies or leaves her area before she has come up to it, or dies as they talk, waits for her again, before the others. With nobody in her area to deal with, she walks back home and stands there as she does. Only the player she fights can hurt her. She is stopped by the obstacles like the players, but does not stand in their way, nor they in hers.
