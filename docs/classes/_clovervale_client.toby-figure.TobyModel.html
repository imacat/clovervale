    Class TobyModel

    The model of Toby, stood where his course has him and posed as he is there, wholly by the step of the server it shows him at.

    Each activity of his plays the clip of its name, from where he is in it: squatting down, standing up and turning around once over their length, picking at the clover over and over, and running over and over at the pace of his run, so that his feet keep to the ground he covers. As he picks after his hand has grabbed at a four-leaf clover he has found, the model plays the clip of finding one in its place, from where he is in his picking, and he holds the clover in his right hand until he has put it away.
