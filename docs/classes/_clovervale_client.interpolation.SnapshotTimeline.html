    readPast

    • readPast(localTime: number, delay: number): PlayersView

      Tells the players and the monsters as they were a while ago on the clock of the server.

      Between two snapshots, a player stands part of the way from where the earlier one tells to where the later one tells, by the time that has passed between them, and is turned likewise, the shorter way around. It does what the earlier one tells, and is there only if the earlier one tells so. A player that has moved farther than it can walk, and than a villager can have pushed it, stays where it was until the time of the later snapshot. So does a monster that has moved farther than it can walk.

      Past the latest snapshot, everyone stays as it tells them. Before the earliest snapshot that is kept, there is nobody.

      Parameters

      • localTime: number

        The time on the clock of the page, in milliseconds, which is no earlier than at the last reading.

      • delay: number

        How long ago, in milliseconds.

      Returns PlayersView

      The players and the monsters, or nobody before the first snapshot.
