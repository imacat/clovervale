    Class TouchControls

    The fingers on the screen of a touch device: one on the stick walks the player in the eight directions of the walking keys, as the left stick of a gamepad does; one dragged elsewhere on the scene turns the view as the right stick does, toward where it is dragged from where it was pressed, the faster the farther, at most as fast as the arrow keys; and two there pinch to zoom, in as they spread and out as they close, without turning. Once one of two lifts, the other turns the view afresh from where it is. The stick and the scene each take their fingers apart, so that one finger walks while another turns. While paused, the fingers do nothing, and no new one is taken. The action button gives the command of the space bar, in play alone.
