    Class InventoryBag

    The bag of the player of the page: whether it is open, the slot its cursor is on, and the menu of what to do with the item there, if open.

    The bag opens and closes with the I key or a click on its icon. While it is open, the arrow keys move its cursor over the slots, laid out in BAG_COLUMNS rows of as many slots, stopping at the edges, and Enter or a click on a slot that holds an item opens the menu of the item. While the menu is open, the up and down arrows move its cursor over the menuEntries, and Enter or a click on one carries it out, which closes the menu and leaves the bag open, unless it cannot be chosen, in which case nothing happens. Esc closes the menu if it is open, and the bag otherwise.
