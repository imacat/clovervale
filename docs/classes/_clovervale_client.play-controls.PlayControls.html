    showDialogue

    • showDialogue(
          dialogue: DialogueView | null,
          known: readonly VillagerId[],
      ): boolean

      Shows the dialogue the player of the page is in, as the server tells, with the villagers called by the names they go by once they have told the player who they are. As a dialogue opens, the bag, the help sheet and the ending sheet close, and the keys held down walk the player and turn the camera no more.

      Parameters

      • dialogue: DialogueView | null

        The dialogue, or null while the player is in none.

      • known: readonly VillagerId[]

        The villagers who have told the player who they are.

      Returns boolean

      Whether a dialogue has opened now.

    showEnding

    • showEnding(isPlotDone: boolean, map: "village" | "cave"): void

      Shows the ending sheet as it unrolls and rolls up, and opens it with the ending, as EndingController.update tells, as the page knows how far its player has gotten with the main plot, and where it is. From then on, the help sheet offers the credits on a page of their own.

      Parameters

      • isPlotDone: boolean
