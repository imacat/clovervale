    Class Gamepads

    The gamepads of the standard mapping connected to the browser, as last read: each button tells once per press; the left stick walks the player in the eight directions of the walking keys, and tells as a button too, the way it is pushed most, once per push and repeating while held after a delay, for choosing in the bag and in dialogues; the right stick turns the view as the arrow keys do, in proportion to its push; and LB and RB held zoom out and in. A pad that is gone holds nothing anymore. While paused, the sticks and the shoulder buttons do nothing, and the buttons still tell their presses.
