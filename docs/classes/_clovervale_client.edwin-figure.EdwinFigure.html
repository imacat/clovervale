    Class EdwinFigure

    The figure of Edwin, the prisoner of the cave, who calls out for help in a bubble above his head whenever EdwinCall tells he does, while the player of the page is in the cave: once both dogs of the cave are dead, as long as he is on the screen, not hidden by the rock of the cave, before he has told the player who he is, and while the player is not in a dialogue.
