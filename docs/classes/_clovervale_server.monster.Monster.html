    Class Monster

    A monster, as the server moves it, in the map of its world, with the numbers of its kind, as MONSTER_KINDS tells them.

    It idles at its home, and now and then wanders to a point near it, if its kind wanders. When a player comes within its notice, and that player is near enough to its home, it chases the player around the obstacles, and attacks it when it gets near enough, until the player gets too far from its home, when it gives up and walks back home. It is stopped by the obstacles like the players, but does not stand in their way, nor they in its. Once dead, it lies there for a while and is gone, and later comes back to life at its home.
