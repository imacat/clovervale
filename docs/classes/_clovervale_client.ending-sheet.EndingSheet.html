    Class EndingSheet

    The ending sheet, a scroll that unrolls to show either the end of the story or the credits of the game: what it shows, how it moves, and whether the end of the story has been shown since the page was loaded.

    Opened with the end of the story, it unrolls; once it has, the player advancing rolls it up, and it unrolls again with the credits; once they have unrolled, the player advancing rolls it up for good, and it closes. The player advancing while it unrolls or rolls up does nothing, so that no press skips the credits. While it is open, Esc, Space and Enter advance it, unless they repeat, the up and down arrows scroll it, and it takes every other key of the game too, so that nothing else on the page does anything with it, and leaves every other key to the browser.
