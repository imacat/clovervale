    Class World

    A world the server simulates, in one map: the village, with its wild dog and its clover, or a cave, with its two wild dogs and its chests and barrels full, and in either, the villagers of its map, each placed in it as it is told to be, as a Resident that goes about its business at every step.

    Whenever a player comes into the world, the world takes the quests of the player in, as the book of its quests, Player.book, tells, and then tells its listeners of it, as a MapEntryEvent; whenever a player leaves the world, the world takes its quests out.

    receiveInput

    • receiveInput(id: number, input: StampedInput): boolean

      Receives an input of a player from its peer.

      A move changes the way the player wants to move, and an interaction makes it slash, drink from the fountain or talk to a villager, as the rules tell from where it stands and which way it faces then, unless it is doing any of them already or dead. A pick, a put-down or a use changes what the player carries, and a reply the dialogue it is in, as a step of the world tells. Any input shows from the step at which it comes into effect: no sooner than the next one, and no sooner than the player has taken as many steps since its previous input came into effect as the input tells.

      Parameters

      • id: number

        The identifier of the player.

      • input: StampedInput

        The input.

      Returns boolean

      Whether the input was accepted. It is not if the player is not in the world, if the number of the input is no higher than that of the input before it, or if too many inputs of the player are waiting to come into effect.

    step

    • step(): void

      Moves the world one step of the simulation forward.

      First, every villager of the world goes about its business, as Resident.startStep tells, before any player moves or picks at the step, and with the players as they stand before the step. Every living player in the way of a villager who pushes the players aside, as Villager.pusher tells, is pushed aside.

      A player that slashes, drinks from the fountain or talks to a villager stands still, without turning, for as long as that lasts. Any other player that wants to move walks that way, facing it, and idles otherwise, on the ground of the map of the world; a villager who pushes the players aside stands in its way as the obstacles do. Each monster goes about its business, as the numbers of its kind tell, and pays the villagers no heed.

      A player that crosses the line where the story ends in the map of the world outward at the step, as the map tells by its checkpoint of the ending, reaches the end of the story, if it is done with the main plot and has not reached it before, in this world or another. The line never stands in its way.

      A drink that starts at the step gives the player back FOUNTAIN_HEALING health at once, or as much as it lacks of its full health if that is less, which is an event unless it is none. A drink is never judged on the world as its peer showed it, so the time its interaction names, if any, does not matter. A talk is never judged either. An interaction slashes rather than talks while a living monster, as it was before the step, is in front of the player and within the reach of its slash. A talk that starts at the step to a villager opens as Talks.start tells, and a reply that comes into effect at the step, while the player is in a dialogue, is answered as Talks.answer tells; whenever a line is said to a player, what the line brings about comes about at once, as Talks tells. A player that dies leaves its dialogue. A player in a dialogue at the start of a step is held in place at the step, and neither picks nor travels. The monsters of a cave whose player is in a dialogue do nothing at all at the step. The village is never held still for a dialogue.

      Every villager of the world then goes about its business again, as Resident.finishStep tells, with every player of the world as it stands after the step, once each player that leaves the world at the step has been taken note of, as Resident.release tells.

      Then the strikes are judged, as Referee tells. A slash that starts at the step is judged as Referee.judgeSlash tells, on the world as the snapshots recorded told it, with the world after the step as one more of them, and with the player itself where it stands after the step. A strike of a living monster hits every player alive in front of it and within its reach after the step, for the bite damage of its kind, and a strike of a villager hits only those of the players it strikes at that it can strike then, as Referee.letStrike tells, before the monsters strike; a player killed by one strike is not hit by another. The players cannot hurt each other. Every hit is an event, which names the monster by its identifier.

      A player with no health left dies, and lies where it fell for a while, neither moving, turning, interacting, picking nor putting down, neither hurt nor chased by the monsters, though its inputs still change the way it wants to move. Then it comes back at a spawn point, at full health, idling but wanting to move the way it does by then, and carrying what it carried, with its silver coins: in the village, at once, and from a cave, by leaving it for the village.

      A travel that comes into effect at the step, while the player could start to interact, takes the player out of the world, if findInteractable finds a way within its reach from where it stands after the step, with the monsters as they were before the step, for a travel to use: it leaves for a world of the map the way leads into, to arrive where the way leads. A player that leaves is neither struck nor judged at the step.

      A pick that comes into effect at the step, while the player could start to interact, gets the player what findInteractable finds within its reach for a pick to use, from where it stands after the step, with the monsters as they were before the step, as ItemKeeper.pick tells. The picks are judged player by player, in the order the players came in, so that of two players who pick the same thing at the same step, the one who came in first gets it, and the other gets nothing. A put-down that comes into effect at the step, while the player is alive, takes the item out of the slot of the inventory of the player it names and lays it on the ground where the player stands after the step, as ItemKeeper.drop tells. A put-down that names an empty slot, or one the inventory does not have, does nothing. A use that comes into effect at the step, while the player is alive and lacks health, uses up the item in the slot it names, if that item can be used: the item is taken out of the inventory, the items after it closing up, and the player gets back the health of its kind at once, or as much as it lacks of its full health if that is less, which is an event. A use that names an empty slot, one the inventory does not have, or an item that cannot be used, or that comes at full health, does nothing, and the item stays where it is. Picking, putting down and using neither move the player nor change what it is doing.

      Returns void
